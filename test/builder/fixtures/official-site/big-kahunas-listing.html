Explore the exhilarating world of Big Kahuna’s Water Park with water slides and rides! From the heart-pounding competition of the Kowabunga Racer, where friends and families race for glory, to the breathtaking heights of the 5-story Jumanji, our slides redefine the meaning of water park excitement. Don’t forget to catch a wave on the Honolulu Halfpipe surf simulator between slides!

Rivers & Pools

  • Bombs Away Bay
  • Humunga River
  • Raging Rhino River
  • Thunder River
  • Lazy River
  • Wave Pool

Whether you seek heart-pounding thrills or a slow, easy retreat, our pools and rivers are designed to cater to every water lover’s dream. Immerse yourself in the tropical paradise of Bombs Away Bay, a picturesque haven for relaxation and family fun. Feel the rush of excitement on our raging rivers or opt for a leisurely float down gentle river rides, offering the perfect blend of excitement and tranquility at our Florida Water Park.
