Sheet stops

The sheet is the app's main spacing decision, and its two named stops are the only large fixed lengths in the stylesheet.

TokenMidnightTrailScaleWhy
--peek 236px — The peek stop has to stand the search field, the venue line and the tab bar at once — or, on a phone with no fix, the locate card that replaces the venue line. Those two ways of spending the same room are held side by side in SHEET_PEEK_PX, not added up, because only one is ever drawn. This must equal SHEET_PEEK_PX in lib/sheet.js, which is the number the map lays its labels out against; page.js seeds the sheet from it directly. It fell out of step when the glance rail left Explore and the rail's 104px came out of the JS budget — a first-paint flash, at the one stop the app rests on.
--shut 84px — The stop below peek. The tab bar and the handle above it and nothing else, so the map has the rest of the screen — a panel that cannot get out of the way is a map you can only ever see a third of. Kept in step with SHUT_PX in app/page.js, which is what the map lays its labels out against.

The peek budget

The resting stop is not a chosen number — it is the sum of the rungs that have to fit in it, added up in lib/sheet.js so it cannot drift from the parts. This is the table a mock cannot see, and the reason a redesign that adds a band to the sheet has to come back here and pay for it.

CSS against the measured ladder
