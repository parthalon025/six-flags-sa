# Host is invisible and follows the best phone

**Host** is mesh authority only — no badge, vote, or toast. Fitness (battery, signal, network, performance) rechecks on join, on failover, and continuously, with a margin so a 1% lead does not flap. Failover-only would leave a dying phone in charge until it actually falls over; twitchy steal would hitch **Plan** last-write, **Overlay** last **Contribution**, and **Ride reports** while the family walks.

Canonical language: root `CONTEXT.md`.
