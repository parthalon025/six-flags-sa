# Family facts on the party mesh

A **Party** is a family product: **Member** records carry height, **With adult**, battery/charging, and an in-bounds **Location** trail. **Location** is situational awareness for the **Party** — not a hide, and not deliberately blurred. The **Party** sees a live position, and the **Place** name only when that **Member** is at the **Place** (an **Attraction**’s queue or station, or at that restroom / food **Place**) — not merely walking past. When live updates stop (outside **Venue** bounds or OS revoke), last-known and the trail stay visible and are marked stale. Device-less **Members** (kids without phones) are full roster seats and are not evicted for silence. Device-holding **Members** may tag them, set height / **With adult**, and remove them from the roster (the **Managed Guest** on a **Profile** remains). There is no location pause: join does not finish without **Location**; an OS revoke after join keeps the **Member**, keeps last-known + trail (stale), and walls on turning it back on. These fields are party-scoped, not **Contributions**.
