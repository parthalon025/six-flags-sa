# Family facts on the party mesh

A **Party** is a family product: **Member** records carry height, **With adult**, battery/charging, and an in-bounds **Location** trail (last-known stays when live updates stop outside **Venue** bounds). Device-less **Members** (kids without phones) are full roster seats and are not evicted for silence. Device-holding **Members** may tag them, set height / **With adult**, and remove them from the roster (the **Managed Guest** on a **Profile** remains). There is no location pause: join does not finish without **Location**; an OS revoke after join keeps the **Member**, stops the live dot, and walls on turning it back on. These fields are party-scoped, not **Contributions**.
