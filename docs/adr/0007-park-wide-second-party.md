# Park-wide truth needs a second Party

A **Ride report** is immediately true to the submitting **Party**. **Overlay** is immediately true on this phone (a **Party** is not required) and to **Members** once you join or host. Strangers see an **Observation** or park-wide **Overlay** only after a second independent **Party** walks near and reports — same-**Party** taps stay in-party, so one family cannot hide a ride or a restroom from the park. Walking near and seeing it is enough; queue-pin GPS is not required. In-party **Ride reports** are name-first; park-wide fan-out and gap **Contributions** stay **Profile**-gated.
