Park Bound: Explore introduces Parkbound — a community-built living map for the World you are visiting. Find Places, real mapped Zones, and the paths that get you there.

FIND WHAT YOU NEED
• See nearby rides, food, restrooms, and Places
• Explore a living World map and follow park paths
• Keep using the map offline after your first visit

PLAN FOR EVERY RIDER
• Choose a rider height to see which rides work for them
• Build a shared Plan for the day
• Check ride details before you walk across the park

STAY WITH YOUR GROUP
• Start a Party with a QR code, link, or six-character code
• When you join a Party, everyone shares their location inside the park so the group can find each other
• Rally your Party when it is time to regroup
• Share ride updates with your Party

HELP MAKE THE MAP BETTER
Side Quests let you confirm things only a visitor can see: a height sign, a missing restroom, or a path. Your updates help improve the World for future visits.

Start as a guest — no sign-in is needed to explore the map or join a Party. Sign in with Apple or Google when you want to save your progress and family rider details.

Available now: Kings Island, Six Flags Fiesta Texas, Cedar Point, and Big Kahuna’s.
