Park Bound loads the live web app from parkbound.kurat0r.ai inside the native shell.

Guest review (no account): tap Continue as guest → allow Location → pick Kings Island (or any shipped Venue) → use the map and guest paths.

Profile review: sign in with Apple or Google from the startup gate or Me tab. No email/password login.

Party review: on a second device, create or join with a six-character code, or use Invite (QR/link) from Me. Location permission is required to finish joining — that is intentional for in-park coordination.

Side Quests / Contributions require a signed-in Profile. Live ride reports in a Party work with a display name only.

Third-party content: drawn maps and Place data are built from public park sources and on-the-ground Contributions; we have rights to ship our rendered Venue outputs.

Encryption: HTTPS only — qualifies for standard export exemption (no custom cryptography).
